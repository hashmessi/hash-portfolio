Case study · 06 · Maruthuva TSL

A two-way communication bridge between Deaf patients and doctors, built on Tamil Sign Language.

A two-way communication bridge between Deaf patients and doctors, built on Tamil Sign Language.

The problem. Deaf and non-speaking patients often can't describe their symptoms or understand urgent instructions in a clinic. Interpreters are rarely available when they're needed most.

What I built. Maruthuva TSL recognizes Tamil Sign Language from the patient and turns it into Tamil or English speech and text for the clinician. The doctor's spoken or typed reply comes back to the patient through an interactive sign-language avatar.

Stack
Python, MediaPipe, Sarvam AI, AI4Bharat, JavaScript
GitHub for Maruthuva TSL (opens in new tab)
Outcome 01
Gesture recognition built on hand landmarks (MediaPipe), with medical-vocabulary workflows
Outcome 02
Tamil and English voice and text output through Sarvam AI and AI4Bharat
Outcome 03
Rule-based safety flows for critical medical exchanges
Scope
This is an assistive communication tool, not a diagnostic system. It helps patients and clinicians understand each other and leaves every clinical decision to the clinician.
